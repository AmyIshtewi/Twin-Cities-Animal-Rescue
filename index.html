Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a non-profit company that helps find forever homes for the animals. We work with rescues, volunteers and local community and businesses.

Our Impact

Since opening, Twin Cities Animal Rescue helped find over 2500 pets their forever homes. We are supported by over 150 foster families and friends.

We are powered by over 200 volunteers who foster, transport, clean and love the animals in our care.

Our foster network has placed over 300 animals in thier forever homes since the beginning of the year.

How You Can Help?

Adopt

Give a rescued animal a loving and forever home.

Foster

Provide housing for an animal while waiting for adoption.

Volunteer

Please volunteer if you are interested in helping with things like daily care and transporting.
